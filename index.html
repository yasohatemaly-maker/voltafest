Official Rules

EVENT TERMS & CONDITIONS

1. Team Registration & Fee

All 16 participating teams must complete registration via the official form prior to cutoff. Roster limit is strictly 5 starters plus designated substitutes.

2. Match Format & Rules

Matches follow official 5-a-side street football rules. Direct referee decisions are final. Respect, sportsmanship, and fair play are mandatory at all times.

3. Grand Cash Prize

The 5,000 AED cash prize will be awarded immediately following the Grand Final match to the tournament winner on October 24th at MVP Lab Dubai.

4. Venue Guidelines & Safety

All participants and spectators must adhere to MVP Lab Dubai ground policies. Appropriate turf footwear is required for all active players.
